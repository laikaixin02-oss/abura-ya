How it’s made

Every roof tile, lattice, window, lantern and drop of sea is generated in the browser. There are no models or image textures — only boxes, curved surfaces, shaders and procedural signs.

The bathhouse is a hollow dollhouse: one corner is sliced open to reveal the bath hall and its upper gallery. At night the windows, bridge lanterns and train windows become emissive, and the water reflects them through a screen-space reflection pass.

Move the cursor over the water to send ripples across the bay. Change Day / Dusk / Night in the dock, or tune the hour to watch the light cross the veranda. Use the Chihiro pad or W / S to walk her across the bridge and through the door.
